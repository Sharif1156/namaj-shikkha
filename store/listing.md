## Full description — English
Namaj Shikkha is an offline-first Islamic prayer companion. Prayer times are computed on your device from your location — no account, no ads, no tracking.

• Prayer times: Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Imsak, last third of the night. 15 calculation methods, Hanafi/Shafi Asr, per-prayer minute adjustment.
• Qibla compass with bearing and distance to Makkah.
• Step-by-step wudu and salah guide: Arabic, transliteration, meaning.
• Duas & dhikr with sources.
• Tasbih counter with haptics.
• 99 Names of Allah with search.
• Hijri calendar and Ramadan countdown with Suhoor/Iftar times.
• Bengali and English, light/dark themes.

Privacy: your location never leaves your device.

Please confirm worship-critical timings with a qualified scholar or your local mosque. The Hijri calendar is the tabular calendar and can differ by a day from local moon sighting.
